01 / Counterparty Credit Risk

Counterparty Credit Risk

Counterparty credit risk is the risk that a counterparty defaults before a transaction’s cash flows are finally settled. For derivatives and securities financing transactions, exposure depends on market values, collateral and netting. The framework below connects exposure measurement to capital requirements.

Inspect SA-CCR calculations from trades to exposure at default, with EU CRR and Basel calculation regimes and a traceable calculation workflow.

02 / Market Risk

Market Risk

Market risk is the risk of losses from movements in market prices, including interest rates, exchange rates, equity prices and commodity prices. K-FRTB-SA addresses the standardised market-risk capital framework. K-CVA addresses changes in the credit valuation adjustment of derivatives, including the effects of counterparty credit spreads and market risk factors; CVA has a separate capital framework.

Market riskProject snapshot

K-FRTB-SA

How do risk inputs become a capital requirement?

Inspect standardized market-risk aggregation across the sensitivities-based method, default risk charge and residual risk add-on. Sensitivities are inputs to the calculation.

03 / Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book

IRRBB

Interest rate risk in the banking book is the risk that changes in interest rates affect a bank’s economic value and earnings. Behavioural assumptions matter: non-maturity deposits have uncertain repricing and withdrawal patterns, while loan prepayments change the timing of cash flows. These two projects focus on those modelling inputs.

IRRBB · Non-maturity depositsProject snapshot

04 / A common review approach

Turn a comparison into an explanation.

A matching result is useful evidence. Understanding why it matches—or why it differs—is the purpose of the review.

  1. 01

    Align the inputs

    Reconcile data, scope, conventions and assumptions. Establish that both calculations answer the same question.

  2. 02

    Trace the calculation

    Compare intermediate quantities and test sensitive assumptions. Locate the source of a difference before judging the final number.

  3. 03

    Record the evidence

    Retain the model version, inputs, reference results and explanations. Document unresolved differences and the limits of the conclusion.

Agreement with a benchmark supports a review; it does not, by itself, establish model validity or regulatory compliance.

05 / About the project

One entry point.
Independent projects.

K-Risk Engines brings together tools developed by Daniele Penza to make financial risk calculations easier to inspect, reproduce and challenge.

The collection is intended for practitioners reviewing bank models and calculations, auditors examining implementation, advisors investigating differences, and researchers and students exploring methodology.

This repository hosts the project overview and website. Each model repository owns its implementation, detailed documentation, issues, tests and releases.
