# 🎨 Website assets

- `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview.png`: AI-generated portfolio artwork in a blue, light-blue and purple palette. Retained as historical artwork; superseded by the six-project JPEG. Its model illustrations are decorative, not model outputs.
- `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview-v2.jpg`: six-project AI-generated artwork in dark navy, teal and electric blue, matching the K-SFTs visual family. Used by the README and website social metadata. Decorative illustrations, not model outputs. Excluded from MPL-2.0 on the same terms as the original artwork.
- `site.css`: responsive website styles.
- `Market_risk_framework.svg`, `CVA_uses.svg`, `CVA_aggregation.svg` and their PNGs: the market risk and CVA method maps of the Market Risk section, added on 2026-10-27 (#105). They are the files of [K-FRTB-SA `docs/assets/market-risk/`](https://github.com/danielep71/K-FRTB-SA/tree/899d6cb34eee29d712d430c9aa547bdf19359360/docs/assets/market-risk) at `899d6cb`, generated by its `tools/build_market_diagrams.py` with CairoSVG and reviewed against the BIS texts there (danielep71/K-FRTB-SA#98); the framework diagram is the map of approaches of danielep71/K-FRTB-SA#103 at `5c84e8f`. The framework diagram is hub-level, like `CCR_framework`; the CVA diagrams are kept here because K-CVA is private. The two FRTB diagrams are shown from K-FRTB-SA at a pinned commit and are not copied here. The website shows the PNGs, 1,600 pixels wide, and links the SVG sources. They are method maps: they do not certify implemented functionality or the scope of K-FRTB-SA or K-CVA. Change them in K-FRTB-SA first, then copy them here.
- `CCR_framework.svg`, `CCR_framework.png`: the counterparty credit risk framework diagram, from exposures to capital requirements, showing where K-SACCR, K-SFTs and K-CVA belong. Drawn on 2026-10-26 at the maintainer's request (#89), as one picture shared with the K-SACCR and K-SFTs repositories. The SVG is the source, shown by the website and the README; the PNG is its export with headless Chromium at a device scale factor of 2 (3200 x 3056 px), quantized to 256 colours. It is an architecture diagram, not a source or a status report: it does not certify implemented functionality, and the risk weights of its CCP table are a summary not checked against the CRR text for this picture. Change it in all three repositories together.

The current artwork identifies six portfolio projects without making implementation or validation claims. Refer to the project status table for current maturity.

To set GitHub's repository social preview, upload `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview-v2.jpg` under repository Settings → General → Social preview. Website Open Graph metadata is configured separately in `index.html`.
