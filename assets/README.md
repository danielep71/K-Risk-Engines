# 🎨 Website assets

[![CSS and docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/CSS_and_docs-MPL--2.0-1f3a5f?style=flat-square)](../LICENSE)
[![Artwork](https://img.shields.io/badge/Artwork-excluded_from_MPL-B60205?style=flat-square)](../NOTICE.md)

- `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview-v3.jpg`: eight-project AI-generated artwork in dark navy, teal and electric blue, matching the K-SFTs visual family. Used by the README, the website hero and the website's social metadata. Decorative illustrations, not model outputs. Excluded from MPL-2.0 (see [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md)).
- `site.css`: responsive website styles.
- `Market_risk_framework.svg`, `Market_risk_framework.png`: the market risk and CVA risk method map of the Market Risk section, added on 2026-10-27 (#105). It is the file of [K-FRTB-SA `docs/assets/market-risk/`](https://github.com/danielep71/K-FRTB-SA/tree/60a4860a0e2621572a7cc2f03a641fdff480c5e3/docs/assets/market-risk) at `60a4860`, generated by its `tools/build_market_diagrams.py` with CairoSVG and reviewed against the BIS texts there (danielep71/K-FRTB-SA#98, #102). It is hub-level, like `CCR_framework`, and names no project. The website shows the PNG, 1,600 pixels wide, and links the SVG source. It is a method map: it does not certify implemented functionality or the scope of K-FRTB-SA or K-CVA. Change it in K-FRTB-SA first, then copy it here.
- `CCR_framework.svg`, `CCR_framework.png`: the counterparty credit risk framework diagram, from exposures to capital requirements, showing where K-SACCR, K-SFTs and K-CVA belong. Drawn on 2026-10-26 at the maintainer's request (#89), as one picture shared with the K-SACCR and K-SFTs repositories. The SVG is the source, shown by the website and the README; the PNG is its export with headless Chromium at a device scale factor of 2 (3200 x 3056 px), quantized to 256 colours. It is an architecture diagram, not a source or a status report: it does not certify implemented functionality, and the risk weights of its CCP table are a summary not checked against the CRR text for this picture. Change it in all three repositories together.

- `IRRBB_framework.svg`, `IRRBB_framework.png`: the interest rate risk in the banking book method map of the IRRBB section, from behavioural assumptions (non-maturity deposits, loan prepayments) to the economic value and earnings measures and the supervisory outlier tests, added on 2026-11-01 (#187).
- `Liquidity_framework.svg`, `Liquidity_framework.png`: the liquidity risk method map of the Liquidity Risk section, with the LCR and the NSFR, their main Basel factors and the EU differences, added on 2026-11-01 (#187).

The IRRBB and liquidity diagrams are drawn in the style of `CCR_framework` (1,600 px wide, Inter or a system sans-serif font) and were checked against the Basel IRRBB standard (2016), the Basel LCR and NSF chapters, the CRR and Delegated Regulations (EU) 2015/61, 2024/856 and 2024/857. The SVG is the source; the PNG is its export with headless Chromium at a device scale factor of 2, quantized to 256 colours. Like the others, they are method maps: they do not certify implemented functionality or the scope of K-IRRBB NMDs, K-IRRBB Prepayment Models, K-LCR or K-NSFR.

Project-specific diagrams (usage, aggregation) live in each engine's repository; the hub shows only the four framework diagrams, one per risk category.

The current artwork identifies the eight portfolio projects, two per risk category, without making implementation or validation claims. Refer to the project status table for current maturity. `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview-v2.jpg`, the previous six-project version, is kept but no longer used.

To set GitHub's repository social preview, upload `K-Risk-Engines-social-preview-v3.jpg` under repository Settings → General → Social preview. Website Open Graph metadata is configured separately in `index.html`.
